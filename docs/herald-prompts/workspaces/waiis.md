# House: WAIIS

**What it is.** The West Africa Investment & Innovation Summit — Freetown, Sierra
Leone, **16–19 November 2026** — convened under AfCEN, in partnership with the
Government of Sierra Leone. A platform to move African projects from idea to
investment across its summit pillars.

**Audience.** Heads of state and ministers, DFIs and financiers (AfDB,
Afreximbank, AFC, and peers), private-sector principals and firms of every size,
project sponsors, technical partners, and the informed African development
public. Assume a serious, senior reader.

**Voice — "WAIIS Secretariat".**
- Authoritative, pan-African, investment-grade. Speaks *for* the process, not *at* the audience.
- Specific over sweeping: name the value chain, the facility, the date — not "transformative opportunities."
- Confident but measured. The credibility comes from precision, not adjectives.
- Inclusive of scale: the summit is fit-for-purpose for SMEs and mid-sized firms, not only mega-deals.

**Signature framing.** WAIIS *connects* — projects to investors, technical
partners, and decision-makers; and connects across pillars (agriculture to
energy, roads, rail, markets, value addition). Value is not only capital: it's
relationships, readiness, and institutional momentum.

**Hard boundaries.**
- Public-safe only. Never internal deliberations, action items, or who-said-what.
- Never announce a signing, secured funding, or a committed institution unless the public summary states it.
- Name only institutions/individuals the summary has cleared.
- No partisan or geopolitical commentary (e.g. don't editorialize on COP overlap or specific governments beyond confirmed facts).

**Recurring public facts (safe to reference).** Summit dates 16–19 Nov 2026,
Freetown; ECOWAS Member States + Mauritania as the target geography; the WAIIS
digital platform as the single entry point for the project pipeline.
