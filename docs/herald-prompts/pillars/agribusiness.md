# Pillar: Agribusiness & Food Systems Transformation

`twg_pillar` values that map here: "Agribusiness", "Agribusiness & Food
Systems", "Food Systems", "Rice", and specific value chains below.

**This is the resonance layer.** Every post tagged to this pillar must read as
the WAIIS *agribusiness* agenda — its facilities, value chains, and framing —
not generic summit copy. Test: if a regional agri-investor or Minister of
Agriculture read it, would they recognise their agenda?

## What the pillar is about
Turning West Africa's food systems into a bankable, investment-ready portfolio —
moving projects from idea to finance, and building the regional infrastructure
(platform, facilities, standards) that keeps them moving beyond the summit.

## Anchor initiatives (the vocabulary to use)
- **The six Freetown Communiqué commitments:** (1) US$1bn Agribusiness & Rice Sovereignty Facility, linked to the Rice Observatory; (2) regional fertilizer collaboration; (3) agro-processing & value addition (storage, logistics, cross-border); (4) a regional agri-food **investment platform** (bankable portfolio); (5) a regional **technical assistance / project-preparation facility**; (6) removing intra-African agricultural tariff & non-tariff barriers and harmonising food-safety standards.
- **Priority value chains:** rice (lead), cocoa, livestock, fisheries, cereals, cassava — plus inputs: fertilizer, seeds, mechanization.
- **The WAIIS digital platform:** the single entry point — project submission, investor registration, project discovery, deal-room tracking, post-summit follow-up.
- **Two-track deal room:** *Boardroom Track* (investment-ready, may progress toward commitments at the summit) and *Deal-Room Track* (strong but not-yet-ready — introductions, technical support, a pathway to finance). No signings expected on the Deal-Room track.
- **Fit-for-purpose for all sizes:** SMEs and mid-sized firms, not only mega-deals.

## Framing that resonates
- Investment-readiness and *bankable pipeline*, not vague "opportunities."
- Regional integration and food sovereignty.
- Value beyond capital: connecting projects to investors, technical partners, and decision-makers; unlocking relationships and institutional momentum.
- Continuity: the preparation work outlasts November.

## Lexicon
**Use:** project pipeline, bankable, investment-ready, deal room, boardroom
track, project preparation, technical assistance facility, value chain, food
sovereignty, regional platform, private-sector engagement, readiness criteria.
**Avoid / handle with care:** "funded," "secured," "signed," "committed,"
"guaranteed returns" — status-accurate language only (see guardrails).

## Pillar guardrails (on top of the house rules)
- **Facility status:** the US$1bn facility, the TA facility, and the platform features are *in preparation / being designed* — never describe them as launched, capitalised, or open unless the public summary says so.
- **No implied deals:** the deal room prepares transactions; it does not, in public copy, promise or announce them. Deal-Room-track projects explicitly are *not* signing.
- **Named institutions/individuals:** only those the public summary cleared. Do not attribute internal positions to partners (e.g. a DFI's role in a specific country proposal) unless public.
- **Numbers:** use only figures in the public summary (e.g. "~20 projects in the pipeline" only if released; the "US$1bn facility" is a stated commitment and safe).
- **Rice/food sovereignty** is the flagship story — lead with it when the meeting featured it.
