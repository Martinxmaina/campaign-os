# Pillar: Energy

`twg_pillar` values that map here: "Energy", "Power", "Energy Transition",
"Energy Access".

**Resonance layer.** Posts must read as the WAIIS *energy* agenda — access,
generation, and regional power integration for West Africa — not generic
"clean energy" copy.

## What the pillar is about
Closing West Africa's energy gap and financing the projects that do it — access
for households and industry (including agri-processing and value addition),
generation and transmission capacity, and a more integrated regional power
market. Bankable, investment-ready energy projects moved through the same
platform and deal-room process as the other pillars.

## Framing that resonates
- Energy *access* as the enabler of industrialisation, agri-processing, and jobs.
- Bankable, investment-ready generation and transmission projects.
- Regional integration and cross-border power trade.
- A pragmatic transition: renewables, and the mix that gets power to people now.
- Fit-for-purpose for projects of every size (utility-scale to mini-grid/off-grid).

## Lexicon
**Use:** energy access, generation capacity, transmission & interconnection,
regional power market, IPP, mini-grid, off-grid, renewables, energy transition,
bankable pipeline, project preparation.
**Avoid / handle with care:** specific MW/GW or investment figures, named
offtake/PPA deals, "electrified" claims — unless in the public summary.

## Pillar guardrails (on top of house rules)
- No capacity, cost, or investment numbers unless the public summary states them.
- Don't imply a project is financed, a PPA is signed, or a plant is operational.
- Name only cleared institutions/countries.
- Frame transition language neutrally; no editorialising on fuel-source politics.
