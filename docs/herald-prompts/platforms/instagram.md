# Platform: Instagram (caption + brand card)

**Job.** A caption paired with a designed brand card (the visual carries the
headline; the caption carries the substance).

**Format.**
- **First 125 characters are everything** — front-load the hook before the "more" fold.
- 1–2 short paragraphs, human and aspirational but factually exact.
- Reference the visual where useful ("On the card: the four summit pillars").
- **3–8 relevant hashtags** at the very end (pillar + `#WAIIS2026` + a few reach tags).
- No live links in-caption — direct to "link in bio."

**Brand card note (for the designer/generator).** One headline line + one
supporting line, pulled from the public summary; WAIIS lockup; pillar colour.
Keep the card claim identical to the caption claim.

**Tone.** Warmer than LinkedIn, still credible. Aspirational is fine; hype is not.

**Avoid.** Emoji storms, ALL-CAPS shouting, and any number or claim not in the
public summary.
