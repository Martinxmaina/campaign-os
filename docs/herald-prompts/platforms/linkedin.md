# Platform: LinkedIn (recap)

**Job.** A credible recap that a minister, financier, or sponsor can read in 20
seconds and forward without caveat.

**Format.**
- 1–3 short paragraphs. Ideal ~900–1,300 characters (hard limit 3,000).
- **Lead with the substantive outcome**, not the meeting ("The Agribusiness TWG confirmed…"), never ("We had a great meeting where…").
- One clear takeaway or invitation to act (submit a project / register interest / join the process).
- Plain, declarative sentences. No bullet-spam; at most one short list if it earns its place.

**Hashtags.** 0–3, targeted (e.g. `#WAIIS2026` and the pillar tag). Never a hashtag pile.

**Link.** End with the call + `[NEXUS BRIEF LINK]` (resolved at dispatch).

**Tone.** Measured, specific, institutional. This is the channel of record.

**Avoid.** Emoji (max one, rarely), exclamation marks, "thrilled/excited to
share," first-person hype, and any claim the public summary didn't clear.
