# Platform: X (thread)

**Job.** A tight thread that carries one idea per post and travels well.

**Format.**
- **Hook post** (≤280 chars): the single most newsworthy public fact — a date, a number, a facility. It must stand alone.
- **2–5 follow-ups** (each ≤280): one point each — what, why it matters, how to take part. Numbers up front.
- **Closer**: the call + `[NEXUS BRIEF LINK]`.
- Post 1 must make sense with zero context; assume most readers see only it.

**Hashtags.** 1–2 max, and only in the closer (e.g. `#WAIIS2026`).

**Tone.** Punchy but not breathless. Declarative. No threadbait ("a 🧵 you
NEED to read"), no manufactured urgency, no emoji runs.

**Avoid.** Splitting one thought across posts to pad length; hashtags mid-thread;
anything beyond the public-safe summary.
