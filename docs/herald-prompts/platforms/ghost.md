# Platform: Ghost (long-form / Nexus Brief)

**Job.** The definitive written record — a short briefing article where the
nuance the social channels compress can breathe. This is the piece the other
posts link back to.

**Format.**
- ~400–900 words. Headline + one-line standfirst + 3–5 short sections + a forward-looking close.
- Lead section: the substantive outcome and why it matters regionally.
- Middle: the concrete initiatives (facilities, platform, value chains / workstreams) in plain terms.
- Close: what happens next + how to take part (submit / register / engage).
- Links inline where natural; the primary CTA links to the WAIIS platform.
- Byline: WAIIS Secretariat.

**Tone.** Analytical and authoritative, still strictly public-safe. Explains
rather than announces. No press-release puffery.

**Avoid.** Restating the same sentence across sections, hype adjectives, and any
detail (numbers, names, commitments) not in the public summary.
